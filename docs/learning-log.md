# Learning log

Your scratchpad while building. One entry per session/milestone. The goal isn't
neatness — it's to capture the moment something clicked or broke, because that's
what turns fluency into understanding (and it's gold for your next interview).

Prompts to answer each session:
- What did I build, in one sentence?
- What surprised me / didn't work the way I expected?
- Which lesson did I lean on, and did the real thing match the theory?
- A decision I made and why (candidate for an ADR in `adr/`).
- A question to take to the teacher or to r/nextjs.
